An Award-Winning 48-Hour Challenge

TUTSAK is a time-loop puzzle game developed within a strict 48-hour timeframe for AYAZJAM'25, where our team (TigerOne Studios) secured 3rd place among 175 participants.

As the Lead Programmer, I architected the core Time-Loop Mechanic using Unreal Engine. The system records the player's actions, location, and interactions every tick and stores them in arrays. When the loop resets, "Ghost" actors replay these recorded actions, allowing the player to cooperate with their past selves to solve intricate puzzles.

Technical Key Features:

  • Custom Replay System: Optimized recording system to handle multiple ghost actors without performance drops.
  • Rapid Prototyping: Moved from concept to playable mechanics in under 12 hours.
  • Puzzle Logic: Designed interaction systems (buttons, doors) that require synchronization between present and past timelines.
